# First MVP scope

- Public discovery of published events and their detail pages.
- Organizer accounts and event creation.
- Draft, submitted, published and cancelled event states.
- Free registration, capacity controls and attendee records.
- Organizer attendee list and CSV export.
- Admin moderation and organizer verification.

Out of scope: live payments, marketplace payouts, native apps, social feeds and recommendations.

Trust requirements: sample listings must be labelled; enforce authorization in the backend/database; never expose attendee lists publicly; verify payment server-side; obtain provider approval before collecting funds for organizers.
