# Product scope — first MVP

## Audience
- Students looking for genuine college events.
- College clubs, student chapters, departments and campus event teams.
- Platform administrators who review organizers and public listings.

## First release
- Public discovery of approved and published events.
- Event detail pages with date, location, organizer and capacity information.
- Organizer accounts and event creation.
- Draft / submitted / published / cancelled event states.
- Free registration with attendee records and capacity limits.
- Organizer attendee list and CSV export.
- Admin review and organizer verification.

## Explicitly out of scope
- Live payment collection and platform-held balances.
- Marketplace payouts or split settlements.
- Native mobile apps.
- Social feeds, recommendations and sponsor marketplace.
- Institutional single sign-on.

## Trust and safety
- Do not publish unverified events as real.
- Clearly label demo/sample listings.
- Enforce authorization in the database/backend, not only in the UI.
- Do not expose attendee lists publicly.
- Never mark an order paid from a frontend redirect alone.
- Confirm provider approval and applicable compliance before accepting payments for organizers.
